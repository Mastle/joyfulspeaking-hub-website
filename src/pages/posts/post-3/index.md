# First One From Japan

October 12, 2025

<!-- ![person sitting at a desk studying Japanese](/images/blog-posts/person-at-desk.png) -->

It has been exactly a month and 9 days since I arrived in Japan at the time of writing this. While I would certainly love to go over the details of how everything has been so far, and what an incredible journey it was simply leaving Iran and landing on Japanese soil as the world is tearing itself apart, my two most valuable discoveries prevent me from doing that:
Discovery number one: As smart and linguistically inclined as I think I've been, I have to understand that Japanese is truly a beast of its own when it comes to language learning. It's gonna take lots of patience and consistency to reach fluency in this one.
Time truly, and I mean TRULY, is of the essence here. Both Japan's philosophy of spending all the available time on the task at hand and my situation as an immigrant who's just arrived and is living on a limited budget dictate that.
As amazing and overwhelming as the events of the past month have been, I have to reserve that for another occasion, since going over all of it would just mean neglecting the Japanese I'm here to practice. Hopefully, I'll have a chance to cover all of it at some other point in the future.
Therefore, we're gonna jump straight into recapping things with the Japanese I know right now, without further ado:

今日は天気がいいです。ニュースによると、雨が降るそうです。
今アパートのベッドの上にいます。私のアパートは静かだし、安いし、とても便利です。
日本に来てから、日本の店の人と日本語で話せるようになりました。でも、まだ漢字が書けません。実は、漢字を書くことは一生できないと思います。それに、家族と 友達に会えなくなりました。でも、インターネットでしか電話できません。
毎日たくさん歩いていますが、まだ太っています。学校が終わって家に着くと、疲れます。
時々考えすぎて、気分がわるい。だから運動したほうがいいと思います。
毎朝起きれば、日本で成功 できます。

And that's as far as I can get for now. I'm planning to post here as often as I can, hopefully on a weekly basis. And I know this is like a pre-schooler's diary, but that's the pain of learning a new language. As bad as it feels to be making so little progress and making so many mistakes, one has to understand that all of that means you're learning, and you have to get comfortable with that fact. Learning a language, especially one as nuanced as Japanese, is gonna be very arduous at first, but I'm here for it, so I'll be here again with my juvenile Japanese writings very soon.
またね！
